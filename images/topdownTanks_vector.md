# topdownTanks_vector.svg — Contents

## File facts

346 KB, a **single flat `<g>` with 846 `<path>` elements**, empty `<defs/>`, **no `id`s, no `<title>`s, no groups per sprite**, and no `width`/`height`/`viewBox`. Content spans roughly x 46–1104, y 42–676.

That matters practically: it's the vector twin of `allSprites_default.png` (Kenney *Topdown Tanks Redux*), but because nothing is named or grouped, you can't slice sprites out of it programmatically — only by bounding-box regions. The PNG sheet is the usable asset; this is the art source.
